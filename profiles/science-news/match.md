# Physics & interdisciplinary science profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding physics, mathematics, astronomy, cosmology, earth science, or fundamental interdisciplinary science.

Typical items include new experimental results and observations, theoretical breakthroughs, space and astronomy discoveries, mathematics advances, quantum physics, climate and earth-science findings, and major scientific instrumentation milestones.

For biology, medicine, or psychology, use the corresponding life-science profiles. For technology-industry product news, use the technology profiles.

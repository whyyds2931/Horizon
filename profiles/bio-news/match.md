# Biotech & life sciences profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding biology, medicine, biotechnology, genetics, or life-science research rather than general technology.

Typical items include new drug approvals and clinical trial results, gene-editing and synthetic-biology breakthroughs, disease research and public-health developments, molecular biology findings, vaccines, diagnostics, and biotech company financing with clear scientific substance.

For technology companies, route product releases and engineering developments to the technology profiles. Do not route celebrity health stories, alternative-medicine marketing, or sensational health claims without a credible scientific source here.

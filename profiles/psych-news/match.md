# Psychology & neuroscience profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding the mind, brain, behavior, cognition, mental health, or neuroscience research.

Typical items include peer-reviewed psychology and neuroscience studies, mental-health research and treatment developments, cognitive-science findings, brain-imaging research, developmental psychology, social psychology, and clinical psychology news with credible scientific basis.

Do not route self-help marketing, pseudoscience, astrology, or opinion pieces without empirical grounding here.
